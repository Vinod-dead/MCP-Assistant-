# Refund & Credit Note Policy

Applies to all customer invoices issued by Meridian Supplies Pvt Ltd.

## Eligibility
- Refunds are available within **30 days** of the invoice date.
- Custom-printed goods are non-refundable unless the defect is on our side.
- Credit notes must reference the original invoice number (format `INV-YYYY-NNNN`).

## Approval limits
- Up to ₹1,00,000: Support Lead can approve.
- Above ₹1,00,000: Finance Manager approval is required before the credit note is issued.
- Any refund above ₹5,00,000 also needs a second sign-off from the Director of Finance.

## Timelines
- Approved refunds are paid within **7 working days** to the original payment method.
- GST on the refunded amount is reversed in the next GSTR-1 filing period.

## Escalation
Disputes unresolved after 10 working days go to the Customer Escalations desk (escalations@meridian.example).
