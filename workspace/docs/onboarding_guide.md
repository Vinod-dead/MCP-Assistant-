# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Meridian Supplies. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Week 1
1. Collect your laptop and ID card from IT on day one.
2. Complete the security awareness course (45 minutes) within 3 working days.
3. Get access to Slack (#general, #finance-ops, #support-desk), Notion and the ERP.
4. Shadow the support desk for two shifts.

## Week 2
- Own a small task from the support backlog and close it end to end.
- Present what you learned about the invoice-to-cash process at Friday's team sync.
- Book your 30-day check-in with your manager.

## Tools you will use
- **ERP**: invoices, customers, payment status.
- **Support desk**: tickets, priorities (low / medium / high).
- **Notion**: process documents and meeting notes.

## Who to ask
- IT access: it-help@meridian.example
- Payroll and HR: people@meridian.example
- Finance questions: finance-ops channel on Slack
